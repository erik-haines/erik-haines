# Erik Haines

**Knowledge Management · Information Systems · Digital Scholarship**

I'm a Montréal-based professional with more than twenty years of experience across technology, financial services, organizational operations, client relations, and consulting, including work with Canadian non-profit and third-sector organizations.

My experience spans commercial, financial, educational, and social-service environments, bringing together technical knowledge, consultation, organizational coordination, and problem-solving. Working across these sectors has developed my understanding of how organizations operate, how people use information and technology, and what makes technical and organizational solutions effective in practice.

Alongside this experience, I have pursued longstanding intellectual interests in philosophy, religion, social theory, human development, and technology. These interests increasingly inform my work in knowledge management, research information systems, digital humanities, and artificial intelligence.

## Professional Capabilities

**Management Consulting & Technology Assessments**

My professional experience includes assessing organizational needs, evaluating technology and service requirements, coordinating solutions, and improving operational processes. Consulting with non-profit and third-sector organizations has informed my understanding of institutional capacity, resource constraints, service delivery, and the challenges of technology adoption.

I am extending these interests into technology assessment, particularly the systematic evaluation of technological capabilities, alternatives, risks, and consequences. This includes questions of organizational suitability, social impact, ethical responsibility, and informed decision-making.

**Knowledge Management & Information Systems**

I develop and work with systems that connect information, people, activities, and organizational knowledge. My experience includes relational databases, structured records, documentation, classification, workflow development, and the integration of information across different applications.

My approach to knowledge management extends beyond information storage and retrieval to the practices through which organizations preserve experience, share knowledge, support learning, and maintain continuity.

**Research Libraries & Digital Curation**

I am developing research libraries and connected collections that bring together publications, bibliographic resources, historical records, and related information.

This work involves metadata, source verification, classification, bibliographic relationships, digital preservation, and the organization of materials for research and discovery. I am particularly interested in established library and information-science practices, including reliable description, provenance, controlled vocabularies, and the meaningful connections among people, works, organizations, and intellectual traditions.

**AI-Assisted Research & Technical Development**

I use artificial intelligence extensively in research, programming, information management, technical development, documentation, and testing.

My approach combines professional judgment, interdisciplinary knowledge, and iterative development, using AI to investigate problems, compare approaches, develop solutions, and evaluate results.

Current methods include relational data management, Git-based version control, software prototyping, technical documentation, quality assurance, and testing across applications and systems.

## Current Work & Projects

My responsibilities encompass organizational development, governance, institutional planning, research coordination, information systems, and digital communication.

### Research Libraries, Information Systems & Digital Development

My ongoing technical work involves developing research collections, relational information systems, digital repositories, and tools for organizing and discovering knowledge.

These projects bring together library and knowledge-management practices, structured metadata, bibliographic references, connected information, search and retrieval, and AI-assisted development.

I am interested in systems that preserve the integrity and history of information while making complex collections accessible and useful for research, organizational work, and public understanding.
