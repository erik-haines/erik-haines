# Erik Haines

**Knowledge Management · Information Systems · Digital Scholarship**

I'm a Montréal-based bilingual professional, independent researcher, and Founder and Director of the [Metapattern Institute](https://metapattern.org). My work focuses on knowledge management, information systems, and digital scholarship, including the development of research resources and systems that support the organization, preservation, discovery, and use of knowledge.

I have more than twenty years of professional experience in business development, management consulting, financial services, enterprise technology, and organizational operations across the private and nonprofit sectors. My background includes client and account management, organizational development, technology assessment, and the evaluation and improvement of business services and operations.

At the Metapattern Institute, I oversee organizational development, research infrastructure, information systems, and digital publishing. The Institute is an independent research and knowledge mobilization organization concerned with health, technology, and public life. My current projects include research libraries, digital collections, and AI-assisted tools for research and information management. This work draws on my professional experience as well as longstanding interests in interdisciplinary scholarship, organizational learning, and the relationship between knowledge and practice.

## Professional Capabilities

**Knowledge Management & Information Systems**

I develop information systems that support the organization, management, and use of knowledge. My experience includes relational databases, structured records, documentation, classification, workflow development, and the integration of information across applications.

My approach to knowledge management also considers how organizations create and share knowledge, preserve institutional memory, and make professional expertise available to others. I am particularly interested in the relationship between information systems, organizational learning, and the practical conditions that allow people to use knowledge effectively.

**Research Libraries & Digital Curation**

I develop research libraries and digital collections that organize publications, bibliographic resources, historical records, and related materials for research and discovery.

This work involves metadata, source verification, classification, bibliographic relationships, digital preservation, and information retrieval. I draw on established practices in library and information science, particularly descriptive standards, provenance, controlled vocabularies, and the relationships among people, publications, organizations, and intellectual traditions.

I am also concerned with the long-term preservation and accessibility of research materials, including the historical and intellectual contexts in which they were produced.

**AI-Assisted Research & Technical Development**

I use artificial intelligence extensively in research, programming, information management, software development, documentation, and testing.

My work combines AI-assisted development with professional judgment, research, and systematic evaluation. I use these tools to investigate technical problems, develop and compare solutions, and assess their performance through testing and review.

My technical practices include relational data management, Git-based version control, software prototyping, technical documentation, quality assurance, and testing across applications and systems.

**Professional Communication & Digital Production**

I develop websites, interviews, podcasts, research briefings, and digital publications, managing projects from initial planning and editorial development through production and publication.

My experience includes interviewing, research, writing, editing, audience development, technical coordination, and communicating specialized subjects to different audiences.

**Business Development & Enterprise Technology**

My commercial experience includes B2B technology sales, business development, account management, and work with software, cloud services, and managed IT solutions. I have worked with Salesforce and HubSpot CRM, Microsoft business applications, and enterprise technology products and services.

This background has given me extensive experience in client relations, business technology markets, account development, and helping organizations evaluate and adopt technology solutions.

**Management Consulting & Technology Assessment**

My management consulting experience includes organizational development, service improvement, and technology assessment across commercial and nonprofit settings. I have worked with organizations to identify needs, evaluate alternatives, improve operations, and assess how technology can support their services and objectives.

Technology assessment also complements my work in knowledge management. Changes in technology can affect how organizations create, share, preserve, and use knowledge, with consequences for professional expertise, organizational learning, and institutional capacity. These relationships are especially relevant to my current interests in artificial intelligence and information systems, including the organizational, social, and ethical implications of their development and use.

## Current Work & Projects

### Metapattern Institute

I established Metapattern in 2018 as a communications, marketing, and managed IT services business alongside my professional employment. It subsequently developed into the [Metapattern Institute](https://metapattern.org), adopting its public research and knowledge mobilization mission in 2024. I continue to direct the Institute on a part-time basis.

As Founder and Director, I am responsible for institutional planning, organizational development, research infrastructure, knowledge management, information systems, and digital communications. My work draws on experience in management consulting, technology, and organizational operations, alongside my independent research and intellectual interests.

The Institute's research programme addresses knowledge management and organizational learning, research discovery and information access, digital curation and knowledge stewardship, scholarly communication, knowledge brokering, and knowledge mobilization.

Current priorities include developing research resources and institutional information systems, strengthening the Institute's organizational foundations, and pursuing opportunities for research and collaboration. These activities support its broader mission of connecting scholarly research with professional practice and public life.

### Research Libraries, Information Systems & Digital Development

My technical projects include the development of research collections, relational information systems, digital repositories, and tools for organizing and discovering scholarly resources.

I work with bibliographic records, metadata, classification systems, linked information, and search and retrieval tools. These projects draw on library and information-science practices, knowledge management, database development, and AI-assisted programming.

An important consideration throughout this work is preserving the provenance, context, and integrity of information while making research collections accessible and useful to others.

### Independent Research, Writing & Communication

My independent research and writing explore philosophy, religious studies, critical theory, political thought, intellectual history, and human development.

Through articles, reviews, essays, and the *Integral [+] Facticity Podcast*, I engage with contemporary debates and intellectual traditions, including discussions taking place in online intellectual communities.

I am also interested in documenting intellectual history and the ways ideas develop, circulate, and influence scholarly and public discussion.
